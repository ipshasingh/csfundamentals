Queue = [start]
Visited = {start}

while queue isn't empty:

    take the FIRST state from queue

    if it's the goal:
        stop

    look at its neighbours

    for each unvisited neighbour:
        mark visited
        remember its parent
        add it to the queue

Hamming distance: 
d(0000,1111) = 4
The number of positions where two binary strings differ


A* chooses what state to explore using:

$$ f(n) = g(n) + h(n) $$

where:

g(n) = actual cost from start → current
h(n) = estimated cost from current → goal
f(n) = total estimated cost
